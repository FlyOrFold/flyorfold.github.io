Sites

Every site in the forecast log, with the limits used to score it. These come from the site list in the forecast-log repo.

None of these limits are verified with the site's club yet. They are working assumptions, with sources in each site's notes. Many sites need club membership, a waiver, or a club contact before a first visit. Check with the club that runs the site, not this page.

Wind directions are where the wind blows from. Speeds in mph, rain in mm per hour, hours in site-local time.
