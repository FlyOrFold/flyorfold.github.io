About these numbers

The chance of a flyable day is the share of forecast ensemble members where the site's wind, gust and rain limits hold for long enough inside its flying window. The line in each today tile shows every run so far for today on a fixed 0–100% scale, with the lowest and highest values beside it. Each day in the strip shows its newest forecast; days before today show the last forecast made for them, not what happened. Distances are straight-line, not driving, from a point stored only in this browser and rounded to about 10 km.

How it works

  1. 1

    Every morning

    A scheduled job pulls the GFS ensemble forecast from Open-Meteo for every site, 14 days ahead.

  2. 2

    Scored per site

    Each ensemble member is checked hour by hour against the site's wind direction, speed, gust and rain limits.

  3. 3

    Logged, not overwritten

    Every run is kept, so you can watch a day's chances change, and later check how good the forecasts were.
