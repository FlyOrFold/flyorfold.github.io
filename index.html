Is the weekend worth the drive?

Fly or Fold tracks the chance of a flyable day at paragliding sites within a long drive of central Ohio, and shows how that chance moves as the weekend gets closer. A forecast that firms up over the week is one you can plan around. One that flips around is a reason to wait.

Chance of a flyable day is the share of forecast ensemble members where the site's wind, gust and rain limits hold for long enough inside its flying window. It covers a model grid cell about 25 km across, not the launch. Check live conditions and make your own call on the day.

How it works

Every morning

A scheduled job pulls the GFS ensemble forecast from Open-Meteo for every site, 14 days ahead.

Scored per site

Each ensemble member is checked hour by hour against the site's wind direction, speed, gust and rain limits.

Logged, not overwritten

Every run is kept, so you can watch a weekend's chances change, and later check how good the forecasts were.
