Good things to write about

A site's limits are wrong

Tell us which site and which limit, and where the right value comes from: the club's site guide, a site briefing, or the club officer who confirmed it. Limits only change with a source.

A site to add

Name, launch location, the wind directions and speeds it works in, and any access rules: club membership, waiver, or contacting the club before a first visit.

Something looks off

A forecast that seems wrong, a page that breaks on your phone, or numbers that don't add up. A screenshot and the page address help.

We can't tell you whether to fly. The forecasts here are a planning aid for a model grid cell about 25 km across, not the launch. For conditions on the day, check live observations and talk to the club that runs the site.
