Rules

  • Sky and Canopy are the two chart series, Saturday then Sunday. The pair is checked for color-blind separation in both themes. Keep that order and never reuse them as status colors.
  • Ridge and Caution are status colors only, always with an icon and a word (✓ Forecast daily, ⚠ Unverified).
  • Text is always a text token, never a series color. A colored line, dot or swatch beside it carries identity.
  • Dark mode has its own tuned steps; it is not an automatic inversion. The one exception is the map tiles, which are inverted because OpenStreetMap has no dark style.

Sky scene

Every page opens on the same scene: a sky gradient (top, middle, horizon), a faint canopy-orange glow low on the right, two ridgelines, and the page color as the ground. Content panels overlap the ridge so the page reads as one piece.

Voice

Say

  • “Chance of a flyable day”
  • “Forecast for a model grid cell about 25 km across”
  • “Rising”, “Steady”, “Unsettled”
  • “Unverified: confirm with the club”

Don't say

  • “Safe to fly”, “go/no-go”, “green light”
  • “Flyability score”
  • Opinions or reviews of schools and operators
  • A wind limit with no source

A planning aid, not a go signal. The pilot makes the call on site.
