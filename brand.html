Mark

A paraglider canopy over its lines and pilot, on a deep navy tile. The name plays on poker: fly the weekend, or fold and stay home.

Rules

  • Sky and Canopy are the two chart series (Saturday, Sunday). The pair is checked for color-blind separation in both themes; keep them in that order and never reuse them as status colors.
  • Text is always Ink, or the secondary and muted text tokens, never a series color. A colored line or dot beside the text carries identity.
  • Status badges always pair color with a text label.
  • Dark mode has its own tuned steps; it is not an automatic inversion.

Type

The system UI font stack, so pages load fast on a phone at the launch with one bar of signal. Headings 600–700 weight, body 16 px. Numbers in tables and axes use tabular figures.

Voice

  • Plain and specific. Say what the number means: “chance of a flyable day”, not “flyability score”.
  • A planning aid, not a go signal. Never write “safe to fly” or “go/no-go” next to a forecast. The pilot makes the call on site.
  • Facts with sources. Site limits show where they came from and whether they are verified.

Planning aid only. Forecasts are for a weather-model grid cell (about 25 km across), not the launch itself. Check live conditions on site and make your own call before flying.
