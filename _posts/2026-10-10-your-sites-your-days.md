The site no longer assumes you fly weekends or live in central Ohio. The home page now shows a two-week strip for each site, from three days back to ten ahead, with a tile for today showing how its forecast has moved. Tap any day to see its [forecast timeline](/forecast.html), and compare it with a second day.

Tap **Use my location** to see how far each site is and list the nearest first. On the [Sites page](/sites.html) you can also pick a point on the map or type coordinates, and tick **Show on home page** for the sites you care about.

Your starting point (rounded to about 10 km) and your site picks are stored only in your browser. There's no account and no server, and neither ever leaves your device.
